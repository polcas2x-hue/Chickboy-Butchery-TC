Trainee Training Agreement and Undertaking

Please read the agreement in full, then check the box below to agree and accept before submitting your application.

TRAINEE TRAINING AGREEMENT AND UNDERTAKING

1. Attendance
I understand that regular attendance is necessary for the successful completion of the training program. I undertake that my absences shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the total prescribed training days.
If my absences exceed the allowable limit, I understand that I may be considered as having failed to complete the training, subject to applicable TESDA rules and the policies of GFFC.

2. TESDA Scholarship and Training Fee
If I am admitted to the program as a TESDA scholar, I undertake to complete the prescribed training.
Should I voluntarily withdraw, abandon, or drop out of the training without a valid and justifiable reason, I agree to pay or reimburse the applicable training fee, subject to TESDA rules and regulations and the terms governing the scholarship program.

3. Physical and Mental Fitness
I represent that I am physically and mentally fit to undergo the training, including the practical activities involved in meat fabrication.
I undertake to immediately inform the Training Center of any medical condition, physical limitation, or other circumstance that may affect my ability to safely participate in the training.

4. Competency Assessment
I understand that completion of the training does not automatically entitle me to a competency certificate.
I undertake to undergo the required competency assessment or competency test upon completion of the training and understand that the appropriate TESDA competency certificate shall be issued only upon successful assessment and compliance with TESDA requirements.

5. Compliance with Training and Safety Rules
I agree to comply with all lawful training policies, safety and sanitation procedures, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), proper handling of tools and equipment, and reasonable instructions of the trainers and authorized personnel of GFFC.
I understand that violations involving safety, misconduct, or repeated failure to comply with training requirements may be grounds for appropriate disciplinary action or termination from the program, subject to applicable TESDA rules.

6. Acknowledgment
I acknowledge that the foregoing conditions have been explained to me and that I understand my responsibilities as a trainee. I voluntarily undertake to comply with these conditions as part of my participation in the training program.

You cannot submit your application without agreeing to the terms above.
